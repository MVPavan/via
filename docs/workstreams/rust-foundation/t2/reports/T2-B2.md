### What changed

- **One dispatcher per session** (`engine/drive.rs` `Engine::dispatcher`,
  `engine/queue.rs` `Slot`).
  - The slot holds the session's sorted queue of unsubmitted turns, a
    `Notify` and the dispatcher state (`None`, `Starting`, `Live`).
  - The dispatcher decides the queue head from one `predecessors` read, and
    runs the granted turn inline.
  - It wakes only on a receipt, its own terminal, the force watch, or a
    read-retry backoff (250 ms doubling to 5 s). The per-turn 250 ms poll is
    gone.
  - It exits under `admission` and retires the slot only when nobody else
    holds the slot's `Arc<Head>`, which acts as a writer lease.
- **Dispatch grant** (`Engine::grant`). It takes the `stop` mutex and is
  refused once force or the Store-failed latch is set. A turn keeps its
  queued count until Store confirms the submission or the cancellation is
  durable.
- **Store-failed latch** (`Engine::latch`, runtime §7). These set it:
  - a receipt, submission or `queued → cancelled` commit that fails or is
    uncertain;
  - a failed event, acceptance or terminal commit, including the existing
    per-turn `store_failed` path.

  Store reads never latch. Once the latch is set:
  - spawn, resume and steer are `store_error`, checked before the
    keyed-replay lookup;
  - every grant is refused;
  - queued turns stay `queued` and are marked unresolved, so reads give
    `store_error`;
  - the stop mode becomes `Force` and the force watch is sent, so daemon
    main runs final shutdown in force mode;
  - `EngineShutdown.store_failed` makes the exit 4;
  - no `session.closed` is committed.

  An uncertain receipt returns C1 §8.1 `store_error` with `commit_outcome`
  and, where uncertain, `retry: same_key_only`. The C1 §8.1 paragraph was
  replaced with the orchestrator's text.
- **Force.** Queued turns are committed `queued → cancelled` without
  submission. `session.closed` rides on the last cancellation only when no
  other turn of the session is unresolved. A forced running turn's terminal
  closes its session under the same rule, never after the latch.
- **Pre-ARM launch gate** (`via-host` `acquire_retaining(…, stop)`). Wire
  passes the turn's force watch, which the latch also sets. Host checks it
  after `commit_arm_intent` and before `launch.put(pipes)`. If it is set,
  there is no ARM: `HostError::Stopped` maps to `WireError::Cancelled`,
  which gives `launched: false`.
- **Start channel** (`Engine::take_starts`, `retry_starts`). One
  Engine-owned channel (capacity 128) replaces the per-request handoff
  permit and T2-B's adoption channel.
  - A start that finds the channel full goes into a pending set.
  - Daemon main retries that set after each receive, and in final shutdown
    it alternates receives and retries until both are empty or the deadline
    passes.
  - A slot still `Starting` at the end makes the exit 4
    (`unstarted_dispatchers`).
- **Removed:** `Engine::drive`, `Slot::turn` and `Finish`, `Queued` and
  `Active` guards, `DISPATCH_RECHECK`, the orphan set, `reconcile_orphans`,
  `take_adoptions`, and `receipt_outcome`'s read-back.

### Merging T2-A (`836b6ce`)

I merged T2-A as `0dc1cf4`. The conflicts were in `via-store` `lib.rs` and
`runtime/sql.rs`, `via-core` `engine.rs` and `via-cli` `server.rs`. Both
sides' behaviour is kept:

- **Store writer loop:** T2-B's `serve_read`/`serve_write` split, plus
  T2-A's `send_commit` reply-loss failpoint on every Core lifecycle
  mutation (spawn, resume, submission, acceptance, event, terminal), plus
  T2-A's `Unfinished` and `AnchorOwners` reads and the paged
  `AnchorRecords`.
- **Failpoint placement:** the spawn failpoints sit around T2-B's
  key-inserting transaction, and failpoint activation is in
  `Engine::open_with`.
- **Recovery:** `recover()` runs before the start channel is taken, in the
  extracted `server::open_engine`.
- **`recovery.rs` (T2-A's file):** it moved to the shared event head
  (`Head::new(Some(last_seq + 1))`) and envelope `(first_seq, last_seq)`,
  because T2-B had removed `TurnRecord.seq`.
- **`core.intent.after_commit`:** a `fail_io` there is now a failed write
  after the commit. The turn leaves the queue at `running`, stays
  unresolved, and the latch is set.

T2-B tests kept as they were, under the new API: turns behind a settled
terminal run; successors behind `unknown` or `cleanup: pending` are
cancelled; a failed predecessor read waits and the turn later runs (now
also asserting that it does not latch); `tests/queue_bounds.rs`; every
`s1_sessions` e2e scenario (F13, F14, F17, F28, `wait.timeout_ms`).

Tests changed because the decided behaviour changed:

- **Removed:** T2-B's orphan tests. These are the three "reply lost … handed
  off once" tests, "a successor waits for an orphan predecessor to be
  adopted", and "an unkeyed committed receipt is reconciled".
  `journal::tests::a_receipted_turn_whose_submission_cannot_commit_reports_its_queued_state`
  became
  `a_submission_commit_with_an_unknown_outcome_fails_and_unsettles_the_head`.
- **`force_stop.rs`:** `force_before_launch_claims_no_acknowledgement`
  (force before the drive, which T2-B then submitted) became
  `force_before_dispatch_cancels_the_queued_turn_without_submission`. The
  pre-launch `requested`/`quiescent` coverage moved to 4c.
- **`s1_daemon_stop.rs` store-failure scenarios**
  (`…store_failure_is_not_a_clean_exit`,
  `…force_after_store_failure_ends_failed_store`): the daemon now latches
  and shuts itself down, with exit 4, `store_failed: true`, mode `force`
  and no `session.closed`, instead of waiting for a stop request. The
  outside write lock is released once the socket disappears.
- **The ignored force-race scenario:** its check also accepts the
  queued-cancel shape.

### Open or uncertain

1. **Unexplained failure.** One of six full failpoint runs failed
   `route_drain::failure_class_deadline_wall_after_hang` at 30.35 s. That
   test runs the fake turn's 30 s wall deadline. Its output was not
   captured. It passed 3 times alone and in 5 other full runs. I found no
   link to this change, but the cause is unknown.
2. **Store failure is sometimes over-latched.** A failed read inside a
   terminal commit (the uncertain-event reconciliation or the head re-read)
   latches, because `commit_turn_ended` does not separate read failures
   from write failures. Cancellation and submission settle the head first,
   so their read failures only wait.
3. **Not done (Task 3, `via-jm4.7.7`):** `daemon/status` health
   `store_failed`, the 5 s diagnostic window, the failure-resolution batch
   for queued turns, closing already-idle sessions at force, and dispatch
   of queued turns that survive a restart (the orchestrator's T2
   integration step).
4. **Coverage gaps.** A failed cancellation under force has no e2e
   scenario; the failpoint's per-point occurrence count makes it fragile,
   and the Core test covers it. The latch's refusal of a new spawn is shown
   at Core level only, because the daemon exits within milliseconds of the
   latch.
