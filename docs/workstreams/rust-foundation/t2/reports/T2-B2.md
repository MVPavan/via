## Open for the orchestrator

1. A drain behind a predecessor that this daemon cannot resolve still waits
   until force. The design keeps this as the Task 3 case.
2. The design runs the submitted turn inline in the dispatcher task. A
   granted-then-forced turn gets `submitted_at` and ends under the force row
   (§4). The alternative is to hold a lock across `commit_submission`, which
   would serialize submissions daemon-wide.
3. The per-turn handoff channel and the adoption channel become one
   Engine-owned session-start channel (§5), which touches `via-cli/src/server.rs`.
