## Verdict: SOUND WITH CHANGES — do not merge yet

The per-session dispatcher is a sound replacement for T2-B’s polling drives, but four correctness gaps remain. The round-3 **force versus submission** blocker is closed at the grant: force before `grant()` refuses submission; a grant before force permits submission and sends force to execution. The **Store-read load** multiplier is also closed: one dispatcher reads for a session’s queue head on backoff.

**Orphan liveness is reclassified, not solved end to end.** An uncertain receipt now latches Store failure and requires restart recovery. That removes the old in-daemon orphan retry problem, but dispatch of queued turns after restart is the assigned integration work. Until then, a committed unkeyed receipt can remain queued.

### Mechanism assessment

- **Store writes and shutdown:** Receipt, submission, cancellation, acceptance and event failures have latch paths; terminal uncertainty has the exception above. The grant refuses after the atomic latch, and `EngineShutdown::is_clean` refuses exit 0 when it observes `store_failed` (`engine/stop.rs:59-70`). In-flight receipt and closing writes defeat the stronger claim that nothing can commit after the latch.
- **Pre-ARM gate:** Wire passes the force watch and maps `HostError::Stopped` to cancellation (`via-wire/src/runtime.rs:203-225`); Host checks it after ArmIntent and before `launch.put` (`via-host/src/host.rs:503-529`). A signal already set at that check prevents ARM. If the check wins, the design treats launch as in flight; the physical ARM send can occur later. The pause-before-check regressions verify the former ordering, not every race after the check.
- **Slot and starts:** Retirement takes admission before the session map and slot; the `Arc<Head>` lease keeps a forced turn’s writer alive. I found no path creating two live heads for one session. The pending set is retried after receives and during final shutdown, and remaining `Starting` slots make shutdown unclean. The late-receipt race above invalidates final shutdown’s assumption that this set only drains.
- **Locks:** In the inspected paths, synchronous guards are released before `.await`; I found no reversed nested lock order.

The T2-A merge resolution is consistent on the inspected paths: recovery runs before `take_starts` (`server.rs:112-116,180-199`), uses the shared head seeded from `last_seq + 1`, and records the envelope from `queued_seq` through the terminal sequence (`recovery.rs:116-140,182-203`). Spawn failpoints bracket the transaction that inserts the key (`via-store/src/runtime/sql.rs:309-356`). `core.intent.after_commit` now marks the submitted turn unresolved and avoids vendor I/O (`drive.rs:714-727`).

The new regressions meaningfully exercise lost receipts, failed submission, both grant orderings, pre-ARM force, slot retirement and per-session read load. They do **not** cover the four interleavings above. The worker’s before-fix results are reported evidence, not checks I independently reran.

### Deferrable decisions

1. **One-off `failure_class_deadline_wall_after_hang` failure: defer.** It failed once at 30.35 s without captured output; the worker reports passes alone and in five other full runs. Capture the assertion and daemon evidence on recurrence before attributing it to this change.
2. **Read failure inside terminal commit latches: defer.** The reconciliation read follows an uncertain event, which has already latched; an unknown head likewise follows earlier uncertainty on the inspected live paths. Separate read and write errors when that boundary is next changed, but this does not explain an additional clean-path failure here.
