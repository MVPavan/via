## Outcome

- The Store moves to schema v6, with the `sessions`, `events`, `turns` and
  `connections` columns that later slices read.
- Spawn accepts `cwd`, `label` and `allow_untested`, and freezes them.
- The frozen `cwd` is applied to the vendor process and reported in the
  envelope.
- `daemon/status` reports `started_at` and disjoint session counts.

This is the DTO prerequisite for S4 and S5.

## Owning mechanisms

These are decided; do not re-choose them.

- **Schema writes.** Store alone writes the v6 columns, inside the owning
  transaction.
  - `stamp` is `MAX(stamp) + 1` in every transaction that changes a
    `sessions` row.
  - `ended_seq` is written with `turn.ended`.
  - `connections` rows are created in the submission transaction and sealed by
    the terminal transaction or by recovery (§3.7).
- **Frozen `params`.** `params` is exactly `{"harness","model","cwd","allow_untested"}`.
  An omitted `cwd` freezes the configured default (`fake_config.rs:44`), so
  the value is always absolute.
  - Validation: at most 4096 bytes, `Path::is_absolute`, and `metadata().is_dir()`.
    Anything else is `invalid_params`.
  - `instructions`, and an unmet `require`, are refused by name through
    `Named::fake`.
- **`cwd` hooks.**
  - H2a: `process_spec(owner, cwd: &Path)` replaces `self.cwd.clone()` at
    `fake_config.rs:75`.
  - H2b: dispatch reads `QueuedTurn.cwd` and passes it to `execute`.
  - H2c: the envelope `cwd` is the frozen value.
- **Tally.** `Sessions` is one std mutex in `Engine`.
  - `open` is seeded after recovery, goes up by 1 on spawn receipt (in the same
    critical section as `unresolved.receipt`), and down by 1 on each
    closed-now answer: `close.rs:291`, H1 at `finish` (`drive.rs:1000`) and
    `finish_with` (`:1035`), and `stop.rs:602`.
  - `counts()` takes `Sessions` and then `Unresolved`.
  - `idle` is `open − closing − active`, saturating.
- **H1.** `SubmissionRecord.connection_id` is filled in by `commit_submission`
  (`drive.rs:1541`). Core names the connection id before `open_connection`.
