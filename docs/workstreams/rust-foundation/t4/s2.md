## Outcome

- **Schema.** The Store moves to schema v6, **defined once and completely**,
  with the blob columns and their CHECKs. S2 writes only inline values.
- **Decoding.** C1 requests are decoded without building any peer `Value`:
  a counting pass, a borrowed envelope, and a typed decode whose free-form
  members are `Box<RawValue>`.
- **Spawn.** Spawn accepts `cwd`, `label`, `allow_untested`, `instructions`
  and `require`, and freezes the first three. The frozen `cwd` is applied and
  reported.
- **Counts.** `daemon/status` reports `started_at` and disjoint session
  counts, with closing taking precedence.
- **Host.** Host answers `live_armed` for S4's `status`.

## Owning mechanisms

These are decided; do not re-choose them.

- **Schema writes** (§3.7). Store alone writes the v6 columns, inside the
  owning transaction.
  - `stamp` is `MAX(stamp) + 1` in every transaction that changes a
    `sessions` row.
  - `ord` is `MAX(ord) + 1`, set once, at spawn.
  - `ended_seq` is written with `turn.ended`.
  - `connections` rows are created in the submission transaction and sealed
    by the terminal transaction or by recovery.
- **The decode path** (§7.3 steps 1–3 and 5).
  1. The counting pass, charged `2 × len(line)`.
  2. The borrowed `Request<'a>`.
  3. The owned `id` and `method`, charged before they are copied.
  4. The typed decode from `params.get()`, charged `2 × len(params)`
     transient plus `len(params) + 24 × elements + 1 KiB` retained.

  No `Value` is built from the request. `dispatch.rs:48` and `:351-358` are
  replaced.
- **Frozen `params`.** It is exactly `{"harness","model","cwd","allow_untested"}`.
  An omitted `cwd` freezes the configured default (`fake_config.rs:44`).
  - Validation: at most 4096 bytes, `Path::is_absolute`, and
    `metadata().is_dir()`. Anything else is `invalid_params`.
- **`instructions` and `require`.** A given `instructions` is refused by name.
  - `require` is expanded by `string_list` and checked against
    `Capabilities::fake()`. The first unmet name is refused by name.
- **`cwd` hooks.**
  - H2a: `process_spec(owner, cwd: &Path)`.
  - H2b: dispatch reads `QueuedTurn.cwd` and passes it to `execute`.
  - H2c: the envelope `cwd` is the frozen value.
- **Tally** (§8.3).
  - `Sessions` is one std mutex in `Engine`.
  - `open` is seeded after recovery, goes up by 1 on spawn receipt, and down
    by 1 on each closed-now answer: `close.rs:291`, H1 at `finish` and
    `finish_with`, and `stop.rs:602`.
  - `counts()` copies the closing set, then counts the distinct unresolved
    sessions that are not in it.
- **H1.** `SubmissionRecord.connection_id` is filled in by
  `commit_submission` (`drive.rs:1541`).
- **`live_armed`.** It is true when some id has a ledger `live` entry whose
  phase is `Armed` and whose `Weak` control upgrades. It takes the ledger
  mutex alone and never awaits.
