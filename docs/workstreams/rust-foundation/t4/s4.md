## Outcome

`models`, and paged `events` and `logs`. `result` and `wait` return their
envelopes as encoded text.

Every reply is charged for the texts and for the encoded line, and a page
counts the whole JSON-RPC line. The CLI gains `describe`, `status`, `list`
and `models`. A3 and A9 are applied.

## Owning mechanisms

These are decided; do not re-choose them.

- **Pages** (§6.1):
  - `budget = 1 MiB − wrapper`, where `wrapper` is measured by a counting
    writer with the request's `id`.
  - A `response` permit for the budget is taken before the Store read.
  - The SQLite thread returns `Box<RawValue>` texts up to the budget.
  - The exact reply size gets a second permit, and the reply is encoded
    once.
  - An item that cannot fit alone is `RESPONSE_TOO_LARGE`.
- **`events_page`** (§6.2): the window is `after < seq ≤ after + 1000`, with
  SQL filters on the v6 columns. `terminal` comes from the partial index or
  from `turns.ended_seq`.
- **`logs_page`** (§6.3): joined to `connections` on `(session_id, id)`, one
  binary-search lookup per reference, and a per-entry global charge of
  `raw_len + 3 × raw_len`.
- **`list_page`** (§6.4):
  - The cursor is `l1.<phase>.<v0>.<t>.<id>`.
  - Phase 1 is the keyset over `stamp ≤ v0`. Phase 2 scans ids 1000 at a time
    with `stamp > v0`.
  - The default limit is 50 and the maximum 200.
- **`session_status`** (§8.4): one snapshot. `process.alive` comes from
  `anchors`, never from `connections`. The size is measured on the actual
  reply.
- **Lane.** Every read goes through `StoreClient::public()`. A full lane is
  `admission_refused` (`STORE_QUEUE_FULL`).
