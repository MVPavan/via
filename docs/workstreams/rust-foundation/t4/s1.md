## Outcome

The Store stops being one FIFO and gains the shared machinery every later
slice builds on:

- a byte pool, with `Payload::stage` as the one raw-unit constructor;
- fenced request lanes with exclusive slots and bytes, including a dedicated
  Latch reserve;
- the minimal Public admission path;
- a raw inbox with group commit, a barrier, a fault sink and a death guard
  that owns in-flight work, with a compatibility wrapper for today's callers;
- the exact command-size guard and the terminal budget constants;
- the JSON counting visitor.

Core tags its shutdown handles. Overload is now refused at the request side.

## Owning mechanisms

These are decided; do not re-choose them.

- **Lanes** (design §3.1–§3.3).
  - Latch: 1 slot and `LATCH_BYTES`. Lifecycle: 7 slots and 2 MiB. Ordinary:
    64 slots and the rest of the 8 MiB. Public: at most 32 slots and 4 KiB
    per request.
  - All allowances are exclusive.
  - The SQLite thread pops Latch first, then Lifecycle, then Internal and
    Public in fair round-robin.
  - `Store::drop` sets the fence, then joins.
  - `DeadGuard` holds `current` from pop to reply.
- **Raw worker** (§3.4).
  - The worker keeps `Worker{batch, current}` inside its death guard.
  - Group commit: payloads, one `sync_data`, index entries, one `sync_data`,
    then the acks.
  - Any non-`Append` command flushes the batch first.
  - The sink is called once per failed connection.
  - The index lookup is a binary search.
- **Compatibility wrapper** (§3.4). `RawWriter::append(bytes)` keeps its
  signature. It stages through `Payload::stage` against a per-writer 8 MiB
  compatibility `staging` budget, then submits and awaits the ack. A refusal
  is `StoreError::Raw("raw staging full")`, today's classification.
- **Command size and terminal budget** (§3.5).
  - `Command::bytes()` is exact, measured with a counting writer. The cap is
    128 events or `TX_PAYLOAD_MAX`, with **no exception**.
  - `batch.rs` `build` (`:205`) measures each cancellation record against
    `CANCEL_RECORD_MAX`, and the terminal's non-envelope records against
    `TERMINAL_EXTRAS_MAX`. It returns `None` (`NotCommitted`) when one is
    over.
- **Handles.** `batch.rs` (`:85`, `:91`, `:165`) uses `latch_store`, and
  `stop.rs` uses `lifecycle_store`. H0: `finish` passes `lifecycle_store` at
  `drive.rs:1009`. `finish_with` keeps Internal.
- **Public path.** `read.rs` reads through `store.public()`, and maps a
  Public `NotEnqueued` to `STORE_QUEUE_FULL`.

## End-to-end test

`s1_store_public_saturation_refuses_the_33rd_read_and_commits_still_flow`
runs through the real daemon:

1. Run one turn.
2. Hold the SQLite thread at `store.writer.before_serve`.
3. Park 32 C1 `result` reads, which fill the Public cap.
4. The 33rd is refused `admission_refused` with `STORE_QUEUE_FULL`.
5. Release the thread. The parked reads answer, and a following turn commits.
6. `daemon/stop` exits cleanly.

It runs through `run_scenario` with `Evidence`.
