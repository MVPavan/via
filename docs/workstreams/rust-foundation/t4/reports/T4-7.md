## Acceptance

- [x] **An invalid `daemon.json` leaves the State directory's Store files
  and the socket untouched.** The config scenario runs 13 invalid files
  (unknown and duplicate keys at both levels, each rule, a group-writable
  mode, not JSON). Each exits 78 with `via: daemon config invalid: <key>:
  <rule>`, and the state and runtime listings do not change. On a fresh
  State, only `daemon.json` exists afterwards. `config::read` runs before
  `ensure_dir`, both locks and the socket.
- [x] **Writes of admitted work commit below the floor and at `wal.max`.**
  The floor is applied only in `spawn_admitted`/`resume_admitted`, after
  the key or operation lookup, and at dispatch (`dispatch_refusal`).
  `Wal::admit` refuses only `Command::Spawn` and `Command::Resume`. Tested:
  step rows, the terminal, a queued-turn cancel, a close and a keyed replay,
  under both limits. Recovery, the Latch batch and Host absence records
  never reach either check, but no scenario exercises them under a limit.
- [x] **Removals** (`acceptance-rg.log`): `rg -n
  'with_writer\(io::stderr|"idle":0|writeln!\(io::stderr\(\)\.lock\(\),
  "\{summary\}"' crates/via-cli/src` finds nothing (exit 1). The
  remaining `io::stderr` uses in the daemon are:
  - the config-invalid line, before any log exists;
  - the lock-contention line, during startup;
  - `DaemonLog`'s startup copy.
- [x] **`session`/`turn` fields.** The daemon's `tracing` calls about a
  session or turn are:
  - the per-turn recovery warning (`session=… turn=…`);
  - the drive failure, now logged in the dispatcher task with its
    `session`.

  The others (client task, re-probe task, the aggregate recovery and
  handoff counts, forced stop) are about no single session.
- [x] **Sol scrutiny items**:
  - A keyed retry of a stored receipt returns before `floor_admits` and
    before the WAL check. The WAL check is at the Store's
    `Command::Spawn`, which a replay never sends.
  - `free_space` is read before `admission.lock()`.
  - A queued turn fails at dispatch with `fail_at_dispatch`, one lifecycle
    write, and does not wait.
  - `truncate` clears `wal_full` only when the WAL length is below
    `wal.max`.
  - The walk runs on the blocking pool under a `tokio::sync::Mutex`, which
    concurrent `daemon/status` calls share. The scenario checks through
    `core.data_size.walks` that a second call within 60 s walks nothing.
    The sharing among concurrent calls and the recompute after 60 s are
    not tested.
  - The open tally: +1 at a committed spawn receipt; −1 at each closed-now
    Store answer (`commit_closed` `Closed`, `commit_session_closed`
    `true`, and a terminal or queued cancel committed with `closed`).

## Deviations

- **`Sessions.open` seed.** §11.2 seeds the tally "after recovery". It is
  seeded instead when the Store opens, from `state != 'closed'`. A closing
  session that the restart then closes goes through `commit_closed` and
  decrements the tally. The value after recovery is therefore the same, and
  no counting point is duplicated.
- **Unreadable free space.** A `statvfs` failure or a step that overran 2
  s refuses new work as `store_error` `not_committed`, with no latch. A
  queued turn then fails `store` with "free space … could not be read".
  §5.3 names only the below-floor case. Refusing is the conservative
  reading.
- **`Refusal.kind2` removed** (`server/dispatch.rs`, T4-5 code). The new
  `ApiError::floor` pushed the reply error past clippy's
  `result_large_err` bound. The refinement now lives in `ApiError::kind2`,
  which `ApiError::data()` already emits, and the rendered replies are
  unchanged. `floor` is boxed for the same reason.
- **WAL scenario.** No `warning` event type exists. The scenario instead
  asserts on admitted work's step rows, a queued-turn cancel, a close, a
  keyed replay and `health`. The WAL growth while the reader held is in the
  evidence (`wal_growth.json`: peak 8,190,592 bytes against a 4 MiB
  `wal.max`, and a 1,091,832-byte WAL cut to 65,536 at its reset), for
  `via-d9o.2.3`.
- `describe` accepts `allow_untested`, which changes nothing on the fake
  route.

## Concerns and limits

- **Merge with T4-6.** Every `ApiError` literal needs `floor: None`. T4-6
  adds constants in its own files; the merge must add the field there. The
  hunks in the shared files (`api.rs`, `runtime.rs`, `sql.rs`, `main.rs`,
  `dispatch.rs`, `check-release-features.py`, `drive.rs` `dispatch`) are
  separate from T4-6's functions.
- The below-floor and WAL scenarios take about 10 s each. The WAL one
  paces 40 rounds by polling the Store's step-row count.
- The via.log rotation is checked only at start, as §7.6 specifies; a
  long-running daemon's `via.log` has no other bound (§16).
- The data-size cache's 60 s is Tokio time. The scenario observes the walk
  counter, not elapsed time, and does not wait out the 60 s.

## Review round 1 fixes

Sol high round 1 raised two important findings; both are fixed. Logs:
`scratchpad/t4/t4-7/r1-*.log`.

1. **An explicit `null` in `daemon.json` was read as absent**
   (`server/config.rs`). Every member now keeps its presence (`present`,
   a `deserialize_with` under `default`), so `null` is refused by its
   key's rule: a numeric key gives `<key>: must be a non-negative integer
   at most 2^62`, and a `null` section gives `<section>: must be an
   object`. Both exit 78 before any side effect.
   - RED (`r1-red.log`): the config scenario gained three rows
     (`disk.free_floor: null`, `wal.checkpoint_commits: null`,
     `wal: null`). The first row failed with exit 9, the fake agent's
     exit, because the daemon started with the default.
   - GREEN: the selector run.
   - The unit `keys_and_rules_are_named` gained `warn_size: null` and
     `disk: null`.
2. **A WAL left at `wal.max` across a restart was not checked until the
   first commit** (`runtime.rs`, `runtime/disk.rs`). `Wal::opened` now
   runs at Store open, before the writer thread starts. At or above
   `wal.max`, it sets `wal_full` and makes one `TRUNCATE` attempt, which
   clears the flag only below the limit. New work is then refused exactly
   as at runtime, and the 1 s `TRUNCATE` retry applies from that attempt.
   - RED (`r1-red-store.log`): the new Store test
     `s1_store_wal_limit_is_checked_when_the_store_opens`
     (`crates/via-store/tests/s1_store_wal.rs`) grows the WAL past a 4 MiB
     `wal.max` under a reader and reopens the Store. The first receipt
     after the reopen was committed:
     `first receipt after the reopen: Ok(())`.
   - GREEN (`r1-green-store.log`): the first receipt is `WalFull`. After
     the reader closes, a write 1 s later truncates the WAL and admits new
     work.
   - The daemon-level scenario `s1_store_wal_limit_holds_across_a_restart`
     is a **characterization test**. It passed before the fix, because
     the restarted daemon's own startup commits (the recovery of the
     earlier daemon's anchor) reach `Wal::committed` before the first
     spawn. That hides the gap end to end, so the Store test is the
     failure-first check.
