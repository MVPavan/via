## Outcome

Pipes are always drained by per-pipe reader tasks, and stdin is written by
one writer task whose writes are pending state. One latch carries a
connection's first failure. `finish` ends a connection under one absolute
deadline and adopts what it cannot join.

Observations flow through a 1024-item, 4 MiB channel with a 10 s stall that
stops the turn with cause `overflow`. Nothing that waits hides a control.
F24 and F27 pass.

## Owning mechanisms

These are decided; do not re-choose them.

- **Wire lifetime.** `WireFrames` owns the reader and writer `JoinSet`.
  `finish(self, deadline)`:
  1. drains until `deadline − JOIN_RESERVE` (250 ms);
  2. marks incomplete and aborts;
  3. runs the barrier and the joins until `deadline`;
  4. adopts the rest into `WireRuntime::adopt`.

  `WireRuntime::shutdown` joins the adopted sets. The per-exit deadline table
  is in §4.6.
- **Stdin.** One writer task per connection owns `ChildStdin` and the offset.
  `PendingWrite` is cancel-safe. Route pins it (§4.3).
- **Latch.** `ConnectionLatch` is a `watch` in via-wire, first failure wins.
  It is written by the readers, the writer task, `next_frame` and
  `RawFaultSink` (§4.4).
- **Stall.** The Adapter's pending delivery owns the single 10 s timer. It
  calls `StallSink::stalled()` once, and `OverflowSink` calls
  `Slot::overflow_order`. Route has no timer (§5.2).
- **Serviceability.** Route's biased order is: force, deadline, latch, wake,
  pending write, reserve, decoded acquisition (§5.3). Core's `while_polling`
  wraps `observe`, `observe_order` and the idle failpoint.
- **Charges.**
  - `readbuf` at `open`.
  - `staging` through `Payload::stage` for every unit, stdout, stderr and
    stdin alike, which charges `max(len, 512)`.
  - `framed` per frame.
  - The rest: `decoded` of `len + 1 KiB` retained plus `2 × len` transient;
  `observation` of the counted size + 512 B; `build` 4 MiB per drive (§2.3,
  §2.5).
- **Envelope bound** [t4r3.9].
  - `ended_record` measures the encoded envelope against `ENVELOPE_MAX`
    before it builds a `Value` (§5.1).
  - Over the bound, the turn fails `overflow` with the bounded failure
    summary: `final_text` is `""` and `structured_output` is `null`.
  - Events keep their own bound (256 KiB of payload). Nothing bounds an event
    by the page size.
