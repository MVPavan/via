## Outcome

- The daemon accepts at most 32 sockets and charges request input.
- A partial line is closed at 5 s. An oversize line gets one `parse_error`
  and then the connection closes.
- Each connection is a reader, a handler and a writer under one `JoinSet`.
- `events` with `follow` streams with no gap and no duplicate, lags by C1's
  literal rule, charges every rescan before it reads, keeps queued events on
  a `terminal`, `closing` or `store_error` end, and releases every subscription's ownership within 2 s of its
  end decision.
- `unsubscribe`, `via events --follow` and `via serve --stdio` work.

## Owning mechanisms

These are decided; do not re-choose them.

- **Sockets and input** (§7.1).
  - The accept loop owns `Semaphore(32)`. The 33rd peer is closed without
    bytes.
  - The reader charges `input` in 64 KiB steps. A line has one 5 s deadline,
    from its first byte to its LF.
  - An oversize line gets one `parse_error` write, bounded to 2 s, and then
    the connection closes.
- **Connection task** (§6.7).
  - Reader, handler and writer run under one `JoinSet`.
  - `Outbox` is a leaf std mutex over the FIFO, `SubState{closed, generation,
    events, bytes, delivered, end, charges}` and the current episode.
- **End decision** [t4r3.5]. Every reason is a trigger. The decision closes
  the subscription, bumps the generation, reserves the notice after any kept
  events, and starts the episode or joins it.
  - Only `lagged` and `unsubscribed` discard unsent data and release its
    `outbox` permits.
  - `terminal`, `closing` and `store_error` keep the queued events. The
    writer writes them, then the notice, within the same episode deadline.
    `resume_after` covers them.
  - An episode's deadline is 2 s and is never extended.
  - The reset rule: when all pending notices are written, those
    subscriptions are retired and the episode clears.
  - If the deadline passes first, the writer closes the socket.
  - A subscription stays charged until it is retired.
- **Follower** (§6.6). Its steps are: read, then register under the
  `sessions` map lock, then rescan, deliver and wait on the `Head` version.
  - **Rescan charge** [t4r3.2]. Before each rescan the follower
    `try_acquire`s `outbox` for `F`, the subscription's free outbox bytes,
    and calls `events_page` with `budget = F`, `limit` = its free event
    slots, and `row_overhead = NOTIFY_OVERHEAD` for this subscription id.
    `Permit::split` gives each notification its exact share; the rest is
    released. Nothing is charged twice.
  - `blocked`, or a refused `try_acquire`, ends the subscription `lagged` at
    once (C1's literal rule).
  - It ends `lagged`, with no back-off, when the Public lane refuses a rescan.
  - `Lease::release` retires the slot. A lease that is lost sets
    `sweep_needed`.
- **Head version** (§6.5). Only `HeadGuard::committed` and `lost` bump it.
  Grep every `insert_event` reach against the §3.7 writer list, and report
  the list.
- **Get-or-create.** `receipt.rs:151-152` becomes get-or-create, so a
  follower's `Head` is not orphaned.
