## Step 2: design note (`t4/design.md`)

Write normative, concise sentences, not a tutorial. Cover only the gaps that add states, bounds or owners, or change failure behaviour. For each one, state:

- **Owner:** the single owner of every new state, queue or budget. Name the task or struct, and say who creates it, who writes it and who ends it. Carry each value in the existing owner, never in a parallel side channel. (T3 lesson: a second watch that mirrored an existing signal was rejected as UNSOUND. When a narrow carrier and the owning one both work, choose the owning one and say why.)
- **Numeric bounds:** the exact bounds and where each is enforced, and what happens at the bound: refusal, `lagged`, `overflow` or `incomplete`. It must never be a silent drop or a Store failure unless a contract says so.
- **Locks and wakes:** the lock order against `dispatch-design.md` and `t3/design.md` §1, and the wakes.
- **Interactions:** stop, drain, force, the Store-failed latch, connection slots and restart.
- **Schema:** any schema change (schema v5 or later keeps the pre-release refusal of older development Stores).
- **Tests:** the failure-first tests that prove it, with test seams (controllable stream rates, blocked readers, memory observation, persisted sequence numbers). No fixed sleep may be used as an ordering assertion.

Contract changes:
- Flag every change to a contract as a numbered amendment, never as a "clarification".
- When you change a bound or a guarantee, grep the specs and both designs for every restatement of it (text, test tables and amendments) and list each one. (T3 lesson: one missed restatement cost four review rounds.)
