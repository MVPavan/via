## Outcome

- A 16 MiB prompt can be spawned, stored and dispatched to the fake agent.
- S2 already decodes C1 lines once, with no peer `Value` (§7.3 steps 1–3
  and 5). S5 adds only steps 4 and 6 to that path [t4r3.10].
- A retry identity over 256 KiB is streamed into a blob from slices of the
  request line, and a keyed replay compares it exactly.
- Recovery verifies every blob, and the sweep removes unreferenced ones.

## Owning mechanisms

These are decided; do not re-choose them.

- **Blob files.**
  - The raw worker is the only thread that touches `blobs/`. There is no blob
    thread and no blob class.
  - A chunk is charged to the global budget before it is copied.
  - `finish` syncs the file and the directory.
  - A row references a blob only after `finish`, in the same transaction.
  - A known refusal is followed by `discard_blob(blob_ref)` for a finished
    blob, and `BlobWriter::discard` for an unfinished one. An uncertain
    commit leaves the blob, and the sweep removes it later.
- **Request path.** S2's steps are unchanged. S5 inserts step 4 between the
  owned `id`/`method` and the typed decode, and step 6 after the typed
  decode (§7.3).
- **Identity** (step 4).
  - The span pass over `params` is charged `3 × len(params)`, transient,
    before it runs, and released before the typed decode.
  - At most `INLINE_MAX`: one charged `Vec`.
  - Larger: streamed to a `BlobWriter` from the three pieces.
  - The prompt is staged from the decoded `String`, one blob at a time.
- **Replay** (§3.6):
  - An inline identity is compared byte for byte.
  - A blob identity is compared by length and SHA-256 first, then the exact
    bytes, in 64 KiB chunks through `BlobReader`, under `admission`.
- **Dispatch load (H5).**
  - `input` is taken with `try_acquire` for the blob's length. A refusal fails
    the turn `overflow` before submission.
  - The load uses `BlobReader`, a running SHA-256, and a UTF-8 check at the
    end. A length or digest mismatch is corrupt evidence.
  - The result is the turn's `String`.
- **Recovery.** `verify_blobs` runs before admission. A mismatch is
  `StoreError::Corrupt("blob")` at the owning row. `sweep_blobs` runs after
  it.

## End-to-end test

`s1_f05_16_mib_prompt_spawn_streams_to_a_blob_and_dispatches`:

1. Spawn with a prompt that brings the line to 16 MiB minus a margin.
2. The fake agent's `EchoPromptDigest` step echoes the SHA-256 of the prompt
   it read, and the digest matches.
3. `blobs/` holds the prompt and identity blobs.
4. A keyed retry replays the same session.
5. The daemon's permit high-water is at most about 64 MiB for this request.
6. After a restart, `verify_blobs` passes and a crafted unreferenced file is
   swept.

It runs through `run_scenario` with `Evidence`.
