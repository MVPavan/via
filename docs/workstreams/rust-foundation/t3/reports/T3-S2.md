## Migration edits (`3ad86ec`) [r3.18]

- **`engine.rs`**:
  - `TurnRecord.store_failed: bool` becomes
    `first_failure: Option<FailureNote>`;
  - the new `FailureNote { site, outcome }`.
- **`engine/latch.rs`**:
  - the new `FailureSite`, `WriteOutcome` (`of(&StoreError)`),
    `FailureScope` and `Latching`;
  - the hook `Engine::store_failure(site, outcome, scope)`. It runs phase
    one now and returns `Latching`, whose `finish`, `finish_held` and
    `finish_with` finish phase two;
  - the behaviour is unchanged: every failure latches.
- **`engine/journal.rs`**:
  - `commit_event` and `reconcile` record `first_failure`;
  - `may_have_committed` is replaced by `WriteOutcome::of`.
- **`engine/journal/tests.rs`**: the fault journal is adapted to the new
  record.
- **`engine/drive.rs`**, every latch site:
  - submission: `FailureSite::Submission`;
  - queued cancel: `QueuedCancel`;
  - terminal: `Terminal`;
  - event: `Event`, through `report_first_failure`.
  - `latch_with` is removed.
- **`engine/receipt.rs`**: the receipt site (`FailureSite::Receipt`,
  `FailureScope::Request`).
- **`engine/stop.rs`**: the forced-terminal check reads `first_failure`,
  and the closure pass's `session.closed` is `FailureSite::SessionClosed`.
- **`engine/recovery.rs`**: the record constructor and the failure check.

## Choices where the design was open

1. **Close watch**:
   - it is a `watch::Sender<Option<Result<Value, ApiError>>>` per close
     generation;
   - a caller keeps a clone of the Sender from its order check until it
     subscribes, so a publication in between is retained [r5.8, r6.6];
   - `force_exit` publishes `daemon_stopping`, or `store_error` after the
     latch, and clears the order before `slot.stop()`.
2. **Claim rollback with an order**:
   - a `cancel` order moves the claim to `Cancelling{dispatcher}` with
     cause `cancel` [r1.3];
   - a `close` order moves it to `Waiting`, and the close pass then
     cancels it with cause `close`.
3. **Joined cancel**:
   - a caller that joined a cancellation that commits replies
     `already_terminal: false`;
   - a caller whose order was dropped unobserved replies from the terminal
     with `already_terminal: true` (design §3.3 [r3.4]).
4. **Absence check scope**:
   - `reprobe_held` is given the session, but Host's `ReprobeReport.held`
     counts groups daemon-wide;
   - so another session's held group makes a close wait out its bound (at
     most 3 s) and report `uncertain`. See the limits below.
5. **Second `Unfinished`** (step 6 [r1.7]): the error is a new
   `ApiError::CLOSE_REFUSED` (-32012, `admission_refused`, "the session
   still has unfinished turns").
6. **Cancel seam**:
   - `core.cancel.settling` is a new acknowledgement-only seam in the
     settling branch of `cancel`;
   - without it, `s1_cancel_during_settlement_completes` could not prove
     the cancel reached the settling step before the release.
7. **Idle progress**:
   - acceptance, assistant text, tool started and tool ended reset the
     idle clock;
   - vendor-other frames, heartbeats and stderr do not.

## Design-table tests adapted

- **`s1_close_cancels_turn_waiting_for_slot`**:
  - the design has recovered unproven groups hold every permit;
  - here, `VIA_TEST_CONNECTION_SLOTS=1` and a live turn of another session
    hold the permit;
  - with no failpoint that creates recovered groups in the harness, this
    is the reachable fixture.
- **`s1_close_outcome_retained_for_late_subscriber`**:
  - the design's force variant needs a caller that enters after force is
    accepted, but today's `server.rs` stops accepting connections at force;
  - the test uses the latch exit instead: another session's
    `store.commit.terminal` fails while the replay is paused at
    `core.close.before_subscribe`;
  - the force variant, and "a keyed replay after a force exit is fenced",
    need S3's serving window.
- **`s1_close_cleanup_uncertain_with_unproven_group`**:
  - the fake's transport loss (vendor exit) does not reach
    `host.anchor.before_eof_cleanup`;
  - the unproven group is instead made by the close order reaching Host's
    pre-ARM gate, with the anchor held at the EOF seam.
- **`s1_cancel_claim_rollback_single_owner`** becomes the engine test
  `a_claim_rollback_has_one_cancellation_owner`: the submission read
  failure and both joins need in-process control.
- **`s1_cancel_is_idempotent_and_coalesces`**:
  - coalescing is covered in `s1_cancel_running_turn_acknowledged`;
  - the transient read failure is covered in
    `s1_cancel_queued_read_failure_is_plain_store_error`.
- **`s1_close_second_caller_waits_without_admission`** becomes the engine

## Intermittent failures

One, in the first default-suite run of the gate, on commit `ab2ccd4`:

```text
FAIL [   2.393s] (216/230) via-cli::s1_turn_control s1_f19_idle_deadline_fails_turn_and_clears_group
Error: "the idle stop came 5334 ms after acceptance"
Summary [  30.413s] 230 tests run: 229 passed, 1 failed, 2 skipped
```

The test itself ran 2.4 s, so the 5.3 s gap between the durable
`turn.started` and `cancel.requested` timestamps cannot be monotonic time.
Both timestamps are wall-clock.

A 60 s probe comparing the wall and monotonic clocks ran alongside the
next suite run and recorded a step:

```text
step at 28.1s: wall-monotonic offset 0.348s
step at 28.2s: wall-monotonic offset 2.988s
```

That is about 3.3 s in total, which matches the excess. This is a WSL2
wall-clock step under load.

Fix (`7c41c7e`):

- when the harness sees its wall and monotonic elapsed times differ by
  more than 250 ms, it bounds the interval from its own monotonic
  spawn-to-terminal time instead (1950 ms up to 4000 ms);
- the failure message now carries both clocks and the events.

The test was not retried to hide the failure. It passed in every later
run: the default suite twice, and the failpoint suite six times.

## Concerns and limits

- **The absence check counts every session's held groups** (choice 4). A
  close can report `uncertain` because another session has an unproven
  group, and it can take up to 3 s to do so. This is revisited when Host
  reports a per-session held count.
- **Adapted tests.** Several design-table tests were adapted, or run as
  engine tests (above); the design table needs the matching edits.
- **Other durable timestamps.** Any test that compares durable
  timestamps across a window is exposed to WSL2 clock steps. Only the
  F19 test compares them across a window.
