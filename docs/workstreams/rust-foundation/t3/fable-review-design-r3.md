## Findings

**Major 1. §6.8 step 4 orders forced terminals before Host reconciliation, but they need its evidence** (design.md:766–768, versus :1032–1033 and `crates/via-core/src/engine/stop.rs:161–181`).
Step 4 reads "finalize exactly those turns ... Then comes Host reconciliation (`Engine::shutdown`), then the closure pass." §7.4 says the batch runs "after Host's cleanup evidence", and today's `Engine::shutdown` runs `adapter.shutdown` first and decides each forced turn's `quiescent` and `forced` from `report.recovery`. Scenario: a forced turn whose Route close left the group unproven; reconciliation would return `GroupAbsent` and `forced`. Committed in step 4's stated order, the terminal gets `cleanup: uncertain` and, when `forced` came only from recovery, state `unknown` instead of `cancelled`. That violates runtime §6.2 ("each count whether Route's own close or final-shutdown recovery obtained them"). Fix: reword step 4 as "Host reconciliation over the collected turns (`adapter.shutdown`); then finalize exactly those turns with that evidence (forced terminals or the latch batch); then the closure pass", and state the reserve Host leaves for those commits, since today's 1 s (`FORCED_COMMIT_RESERVE`) is smaller than one of §7.4's 2 s bounds.

**Major 2. Close waiters have an unstated subscribe order and no publisher on a force or latch exit** (design.md:433–435, :449–451, :468, :516–517, :518–520).
Steps 2, 5 and 7 now say "release `admission`, then wait on the slot's close watch". If the receiver is created after the release, the dispatcher can publish and exit in between. A `watch::Receiver` created after the publish marks that value seen, so `changed()` waits for a next change that never comes while the slot stays leased (a forced turn's `Arc<Head>` keeps it unretired). Separately, under force or the latch the dispatcher's force check comes first and it exits via `slot.stop()` without running the close pass; the text promises waiters `daemon_stopping` "once the daemon is finalized" or `store_error`, but names no publisher and, unlike §3.3's cancel waiter, does not tell the close waiter to watch `finalized`. Result: the waiter hangs until process exit and the client sees EOF, not the promised error. Fix: subscribe to the close watch under `admission`, then release, then await; and have the dispatcher publish `daemon_stopping` (force) or `store_error` (latch) on the close watch before `slot.stop()`. Add both to §1 Wakes.

**Minor 3. The raw thread's `Unavailable` is not classified by r3.9** (design.md:813–822; `crates/via-store/src/runtime.rs:349–350`, `runtime/raw.rs:38`).
Row 6 says raw writes have no uncertain outcome. A dead raw thread (`try_send Disconnected`, or a dropped raw reply) then fails every later turn through row 6 while `health` stays `healthy`. That is the same failure mode decision 9 closed for the writer thread. Fix: raw `try_send Disconnected` and a dropped raw reply are `WriterLost` and latch; only raw `Full` and I/O errors are row 6.

**Minor 4. Entry timing is stated two ways** (design.md:745–746 versus :1005–1010).
§6.8 says daemon main calls `enter_final_shutdown` "when it leaves the serve loop"; §7.4 says the pipeline runs concurrently with the diagnostic window while daemon main still serves. Fix: "when it stops accepting work: drain end, idle expiry, force acceptance, or the latch's phase two; on the latch path the window then serves concurrently". Also state that the re-check at :747–751 only feeds pipeline step 2 and never returns to serving.

**Minor 5. The durable closing set is said to be taken with no other lock held, but it is filled and emptied under `admission`** (design.md:74–76 versus :461, :481). Same is true of today's `force_sessions`. Fix: "short `std` mutexes, never held across an `.await` and never nested with another `std` lock; they may be taken under `admission`".

**Minor 6. Two seams and two tests cannot prove what they claim as written** (design.md:1286, :1322, :1324, :1325).
The `daemon.shutdown.entry` seam is "before `final_shutdown` is set". If it sits under `admission`, the close in `s1_close_at_final_shutdown_entry_refused` merely queues on `admission` and the start-drain race is never created. Fix: place the seam before `admission` is taken. `s1_cancel_wait_across_force_handoff` ("no reply while paused") and `s1_close_failed_closed_keeps_closing_count` ("idle exit does not fire") are negative claims that need a timed check. Fix: state the technique, namely the reply timestamp ordered after the release acknowledgement, and for idle exit a bound of twice `VIA_TEST_IDLE_EXIT_MS` measured from the failed `Closed`'s acknowledgement.
